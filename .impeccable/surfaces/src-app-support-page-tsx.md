# Support page (src/app/support/page.tsx)

Scope: the public support route only. Mode: Operate. The visitor sends one message. The page inherits the home board and does not invent a second palette.

Audience: a person who already saw the landing page and opened Support. Job: send a message to Pamiac support with the email they sign in with. Action: Send to support. Proof: the existing copy only. No new claims.

Constraints: keep the form behavior, validation, success and error states, and the current English copy. Do not restyle the home page. The desk (paper, ink, teal) stays everywhere except this route.

## Direction contract

THESIS: support is the same lit circuit board as the landing page, and the only job on it is the message. It refuses the paper auth card and a second marketing hero.

OWN-WORLD: the home board, unchanged. Near-black ground, dim olive traces, lime #b9f542, Outfit for the title, a translucent panel with a 1px lime hairline and an offset shadow. Fields are dark with a lime focus ring. The send control is the one lime-filled pill.

STORY: the visitor arrives from Support in the header, reads that the message goes to Pamiac support, enters the sign-in email and the message, and sends it. Success stays in the same panel.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the home header over the circuit board; one centered panel, about 460px, with the lime Support label, the sans title Contact support, the two existing lines, email, message, and a full-width lime Send to support pill. The home footer line closes the page.

FORM: pinned by the request to match the root page. No concept roll. Seed: none (brief-pinned).

Signature interaction: the shared board already pulses along its traces and the pads breathe; both stop under prefers-reduced-motion. Field focus and the send control use lime. No entrance animation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
